Finding your way

  • Home page. Search by topic or by number (type 3 to find lecture 03), filter by kind, and tick Topic details to see a one-line description of every page. Press / to jump to the search box.
  • Top bar. The switcher jumps to any page; Prev and Next walk through the lectures in order; the theme button cycles system, light and dark.
  • Section rail. On wide screens the list of sections stays on the left and highlights where you are; on phones it folds into a Sections menu at the top.
  • Progress. Each lecture section ends with Mark section as reviewed. Your progress shows in the rail and on the home page, and the home page offers a Continue link to the last page you read. It is stored only in this browser.

Sources and how they are cited

Each lecture has its own set of slides and four pages of a printable reader (reader.pdf). The notes cite both.

  • A slide reference such as P01 s1 is a button: it opens that slide beside the text, with Prev and Next to page through the deck. Ranges such as P01 s1–2 are highlighted while you are inside them.
  • A reader reference such as Reader §1.1 shows its page and opens that page of the PDF.
  • The line under each section heading lists the sources for the whole section.

Interactive parts

  • Widgets (labelled Try it) respond as you type, drag or click. Most have presets: buttons that load an interesting case, each with a one-line note on why it is interesting.
  • Steppers reveal an argument one step at a time.
  • Worked-example cards fold their solutions away; a Load button puts the example into the matching widget.
  • Self-tests at the end of every lecture remember your answers; Reset clears them.
  • The summary sheets page collects every lecture's sheet for review and printing.
