• Conditional probability: P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B), only when P(B) > 0. Keep the outcomes in B, rescale. It is a probability in A.
  • Not symmetric: P(A | B) and P(B | A) are usually different numbers.
  • Multiplication / chain rule: P(A ∩ B) = P(B)P(A | B); P(A ∩ B ∩ C) = P(A)P(B | A)P(C | A ∩ B). Two aces: 4/52 · 3/51 = 1/221.
  • Independence: P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B), i.e. P(A | B) = P(A). Check it with numbers. Disjoint events with positive probabilities are never independent.
  • Total probability: for a partition B₁, B₂, …, P(A) = ∑ P(A | Bk)P(Bk).
  • Bayes’ rule: P(B | A) = P(A | B)P(B) / P(A) (slide s8 swaps P(A) and P(B): erratum). Prior, likelihood, posterior.
  • Tree: multiply along paths, add paths with the same result, divide one path by that sum.
  • Base rates: always ask for P(D). Treating P(+ | D) as P(D | +) is the base-rate fallacy.
