• Sample space Ω: all outcomes of an experiment. Event: a subset of Ω.
  • Complement E′ (not E), union E ∪ F (at least one), intersection E ∩ F (both); disjoint: E ∩ F = ∅.
  • Equally likely outcomes: P(E) = |E| / |Ω|. Two dice: |Ω| = 36, P(sum 7) = 1/6.
  • Axioms: P(E) ≥ 0, P(Ω) = 1, disjoint events add.
  • Complement rule P(E′) = 1 − P(E); inclusion–exclusion P(E ∪ F) = P(E) + P(F) − P(E ∩ F).
  • Multiplication rule: multiply the number of choices at each step (the count must not depend on earlier choices).
  • “At least one”: count the complement. P(a six in four rolls) = 1 − (5/6)⁴ = 671/1296 ≈ 0.518.
