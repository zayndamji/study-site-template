03 Shapes: a swinging gate

A gate turns on a hinge. The shaded region is the floor the gate sweeps, the gate itself is a rod, the hinge a pin, and a hollow ring marks the latch. Here the page uses its own class names (swept, gate, hinge, latch), mapped onto those shapes and colours with the markNames and colorNames options.

Try it · Open the gate

Drag the end of the gate. It turns red past 120°, where it would hit the wall.
