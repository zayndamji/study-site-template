01 A turnstile, step by step

A coin-operated turnstile is locked until a coin goes in; one push lets a person through and locks it again. Type a sequence of coins (c) and pushes (p), then step through it. The machine accepts when the turnstile ends up locked.

02 Guessing machines

A machine may have several moves for one symbol, or free moves (ε) that read nothing; it then follows all of them at once. This one looks for the word ab anywhere in the input.

03 Many inputs at once

A page script can run the turnstile above on a whole list of inputs and show a verdict for each. The pieces it builds carry the package's classes (sm-verdict, sm-status), and the classStyles option styles them like the simulator's own.
