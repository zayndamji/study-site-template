01 How fast things grow

Four standard growth curves. Move across the chart (or focus it and use the arrow keys) to read every curve at one input size, press a legend entry to hide it, and drag the slider to see what happens for larger inputs. On a linear axis the fastest curve soon hides the others; the log scale button brings them back into view.

02 Counted steps and a best match

A data series is a list of [n, count] pairs, here a made-up tally of steps at six input sizes. With "fit": true the chart compares the tally with the standard curves and draws the closest one, scaled to the data, as a dashed line.
