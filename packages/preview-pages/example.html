01 Provisional pages

Draft. This page is provisional and will be revised.

Give a ready page a "statusLabel" in site.json, for example "Draft", and it is built as usual (page switcher, Prev/Next, summaries) with two additions: the label appears beside its title on the home page, and a banner like the one above sits under the page title. Remove the label once the page is final.

A page can replace the banner text with "statusNote", which takes the same fields as the banner option.
