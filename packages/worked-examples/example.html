Edit the amounts, or load one of the examples below. The balance after n years is P(1 + r)n.

Example 1Doubling timewarm-up
How much is 100 worth after 7 years at 10% a year?
Show solution
  1. Each year multiplies the balance by 1.1.
  2. After 7 years: 100 × 1.17 ≈ 100 × 1.949 = 194.87.
  3. So the money almost doubles — the "rule of 72" estimate is 72 / 10 ≈ 7 years.
